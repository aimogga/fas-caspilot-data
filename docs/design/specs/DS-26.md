# DS-26 — Grant match card fits its column

Source docs: issue #310 (DS-26a); QA comment on #306 (2026-10-17) with evidence `docs/design/reviews/F-504d/before/staff-grants-matches-1024.png` and `after/staff-grants-matches-1024-pass.png`; DS-12a/b (the match list and panel); F-504d (fit bands); `.claude/skills/design-polish/references/*`.
**Depends on:** #306 (F-504d, merged). Both faults were already on main before #306.

## Purpose and users
Staff scan `/staff/grants/matches` to choose which grants to work on. At 1024 the sticky "How we matched" aside takes 20–24 rem, which leaves the list about 300 px wide. The card still put the score tile beside the body, so the body was about 140 px wide. "Draft a response strategy" ran past the card's right edge, and long flag chips (`whitespace-normal`) wrapped into pills up to 86 px tall.

| Role | Gets |
|---|---|
| staff, admin | Cards that fit their column at every width; one-line flag chips with the full words in their card |

## Scope (sub-packet a; the whole packet)
- `components/grants/MatchList.tsx`: the list `ol` is a size container. A card goes two-column (score tile beside the body) only from 1024 **and** when the list is at least 36 rem wide (`lg:@xl:`). Otherwise the score row (tile plus band badge) sits above the body, the same layout as at 375. The body is a named container (`@container/body`).
- Flag chips: `max-w-full`, and the words sit in a `truncate` span (one line, ellipsis). The chip's `ExplainCard` gains an "In full" row whenever the chip words differ from the stored reason, so the full words are never lost.
- `components/grants/DraftStrategyButton.tsx`: in a card body narrower than 13 rem the words "a response" are visually hidden (`@max-[13rem]/body:sr-only`), so the label reads "Draft strategy" while the accessible name stays "Draft a response strategy". The form is `min-w-0 max-w-full` and wraps under "How we matched" in the existing `flex-wrap` row.
- Out of scope: `FitBandBadge` (F-504d, unchanged); scores, bands and the panel; `MatchPanel` source chips.

## Layouts
| Viewport | Card |
|---|---|
| 375 | unchanged: score row (tile + band) above the body; buttons stack and wrap; 44 px targets |
| 768 | unchanged (below `lg`): stacked, panel inside the card |
| 1024 | list ≈ 300 px (< 36 rem): stacked like 375, body ≈ 250 px; both buttons fit on separate lines |
| 1440 | list ≈ 750 px (≥ 36 rem): two-column as before |

## Components (existing only)
`Badge`, `ExplainCard`, `Hint`, `Button`, Tailwind v4 container queries. New: none. No token, no CSS file, no kit file touched.

## Exact copy
- Strategy button: visible `Draft a response strategy`, or `Draft strategy` in a body under 13 rem. Accessible name is always `Draft a response strategy`.
- Flag card row: `In full` · the chip words (only when they differ from `Stored reason`).

## Trust rules
No number, score or band changes. Chip words come from `flagWords` as before. The card still shows the stored reason and where it was checked from.

## Accessibility
Colour is never the only signal: chips keep their glyph and words. Targets stay 44 px below 1024 (`Button` `sm`, explain triggers `min-h-11`). The truncated chip is a button whose card (hover, focus, tap) carries the full words. axe serious/critical = 0 at 768, 1024 and 1440.
