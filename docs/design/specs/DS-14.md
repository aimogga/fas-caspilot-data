# DS-14 — Sign in, Forbidden and the loading skeletons: every chip explains itself, the H1 takes focus, every main route has a skeleton
Source docs: `docs/design/POLISH_PLAN.md` row DS-14; `docs/design/fas-screen-plan-part2.md` §4 P0-1 (Sign in) and P0-3 (Forbidden); mockups `docs/design/mockups/p0-login.png`, `p0-login-375.png`, `p0-403.png`, `p0-403-375.png`; `docs/design/targets/visual-direction.md` (shared type steps); `.claude/skills/design-polish/references/*`; DS-04 (status pages), DS-09 (explain kit), DS-13a (spec format).
**Depends on:** DS-09 explain kit on main. **Budget:** ≤ 400 hand-written lines (D-012). **Before shots:** `docs/design/reviews/DS-14/before/login-{375,1024,1440}.png` (see Open questions 1). **After shots:** taken by the supervisor from the preview (`/login` signed out; `/403` as board).

## Purpose and users
The two pages everyone meets before any data, and the moment between a click and a page. Sign in must read as calm and finished, and its one example number must say that it is an example. Forbidden must say why, in the reader's own role, and put the keyboard where the answer is. A loading screen must look like the page that is coming, and say so to a screen reader.

| Role | Gets |
|---|---|
| signed out | `/login`: the form, the example card with a hint on "Example" and a card on its footer, the preview badge with a hint |
| board_viewer (and any role) | `/403`: the H1 focused on load, the role sentence with a hint, the requested path in mono, no claim the app cannot keep |
| staff, admin, board_viewer | a skeleton on `/staff`, `/dashboard`, `/need-map`, `/board` and `/staff/grants/matches` while the server renders |

## Scope (single sub-packet)
- `/login`: `Hint` on the "Example" chip; the sample footer is an `ExplainCard` trigger (term "Example figure") with Source, Period, n and As of rows; `Hint` on the "Synthetic data · preview" badge; the example card becomes a real card (`bg-card`, 1 px border, `shadow-sm`, 24 px padding, 36 px tabular numeral) and is no longer `aria-hidden`; the error alert keeps the generic copy (see Decisions); the submit stays 48 px (already in `globals.css`).
- `/403`: `components/status/FocusHeading.tsx` (client `h1`, `tabIndex -1`, focus on mount); `StatusCard` gains `focusTitle` and renders the icon in the `EmptyState` pattern (40 px glyph, muted, no tile); the role sentence carries a `Hint`; the requested path stays mono; nothing says the attempt was logged.
- `loading.tsx` for the five main routes through one `components/status/PageSkeleton.tsx` (header bars + blocks sized to each page's cards; `role="status"` (no `aria-busy`, so the sentence is read), sr-only "Loading …" sentence; `motion-reduce:animate-none`).
- Out of scope: "Forgot password?" (F-605), "Ask an admin for access" (no email path), audit row on a 403, globals edits, skeletons for `/data-freshness` and `/methodology` (DS-16).

## Exact copy
- Example chip hint: `An example of how a number looks once you sign in. It is not a real figure.`
- Example footer card: title `Example figure`; plain `A made-up number that shows how every figure in FAS Evidence carries its source, period, count and date. Nothing here comes from a family record.`; rows Source `FAS records (example)`, Period `Q3 2026`, n `64`, As of `Sep 2026`.
- Badge hint: `This preview runs on made-up records. No real family data is here.`
- 403 role sentence: `You're signed in as <role>.` with the hint `<role> is the role on your account. It decides which areas you can open; an FAS admin can change it.`
- Skeleton sentence: `Loading <page name>. The page appears in a moment.` where page name is `your home`, `the team dashboard`, `the county need map`, `the board summary`, `grant matches`.

## States

## Trust rules
No new number. The example number stays an example and says so on hover, focus and tap and in its own words. No claim of logging on the 403 because no code writes that row. No "Forgot password?" because reset does not exist. Synthetic badge explained.

## Accessibility

## Decisions
1. The mockup's lockout sentence ("After 5 attempts, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.") is not adopted: Supabase rate-limits differently and the app has no lockout; the existing e2e test forbids those words. The rate-limited copy stays "Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again."
2. The footer card uses a page-local term ("Example figure") through `ExplainCard` rather than `Provenance`, because `Provenance` would describe "FAS records" as a real load; the rows are the same four a `Provenance` shows.
3. The icon tile on status cards gives way to the `EmptyState` 40 px glyph pattern on every status page (403, 404, 500 share `StatusCard`).
4. "Every sign-in is logged." stays: Supabase Auth keeps its own audit entries for sign-ins. "This attempt was logged." on the 403 is not shown: no code inserts an `audit_log` row there.
5. The `signed-out-bar` and login grid stay on their `globals.css` rules (kit file); the example card moves to utilities under a new slot instead.

## Open questions
1. The committed before shots `docs/design/reviews/DS-14/before/login-*.png` show `/board`, not `/login`: `pnpm shots` signed in as board and `/login` redirected. Retake them signed out for the review page.
2. A real 403 audit row (`audit_log` insert from the guard) would let the page say the attempt was logged, as the screen plan asks; that is an engineer packet.
3. "Ask an admin for access" needs an outbound path (George gate: email); left out.
