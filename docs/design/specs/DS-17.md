# DS-17 — Evidence packets: every figure explains itself, controls and downloads in words, sections with a headline in words, page numbers in print
Source docs: `docs/design/targets/visual-direction.md` (the bar; the Board artboard is the nearest target: eyebrow → serif finding → support → source line); `docs/design/POLISH_PLAN.md` row DS-17; `docs/design/fas-screen-plan-part2.md` §4 (packet builder row, F-403/404); no packet mockup, so the board sections and the DS-13a/b print sheet are the pattern; `.claude/skills/design-polish/references/*` (bar, plain language, audit, evidence); DS-09 (explain kit); DS-11a (`ExplainLayer`); DS-13a/b (Letter print, `@page` page numbers, fixed footer); DS-15a (`m4Cell` attribute builder pattern).
**Depends on:** DS-09 kit, DS-11a `ExplainLayer`, DS-13b print sheet on main. **Budget:** ≤ 400 hand-written lines (D-012). **Before and after shots:** taken by the supervisor from production and the preview (this shell has no Docker, Supabase or preview access).

## Purpose and users
The page where staff build the grant attachment or the board packet and print it. A case manager choosing a variant and county must know what each menu does and what each download holds before clicking; a reader of the packet (staff on screen, a funder or board member on paper) must be able to hover, focus or tap any figure tile and read what it means, where it comes from, the as-of month and why a value is hidden, and must find the same words on the tile and in the section source line so the card is never the only place.

| Role | Gets |
|---|---|
| staff, admin | `/staff/packets` with the hover layer on every figure tile, hints on the Variant and County menus and on both downloads, sections with an eyebrow and a serif headline in words, page numbers in print |
| board_viewer | 403 (unchanged; the board downloads from `/board`) |

## Scope (single packet)
- `lib/packets/words.ts` (pure): `PACKET_TERMS` (outcome change, share improved, average rate per hour, weight, service counties, a generic indicator), `figureTermId(f)` (metric titles → `m1`…`m6`; need indicators → the `ind:<code>` term from `lib/need-map/terms.ts` by title; otherwise a per-label fallback), `packetTerms(packet)` (the record one `ExplainLayer` reads, including per-label fallbacks titled with the label), `figureCell(f)` → `data-term/value/source/asof/hidden` (`fewer than 11` → the privacy sentence, `hidden` → the complementary sentence, `Not available yet` → the not-available sentence; never a value for a hidden figure), `sectionLead(paragraphs)` (first sentence as the headline, the rest as body), `sectionEyebrow(i, total, title)`, `CONTROL_HINTS` (Variant, County), `DOWNLOAD_HINTS` (Word, CSV).
- `components/packets/PacketView.tsx`: the six sections sit inside one `ExplainLayer`; each section gets an eyebrow (`Section 1 of 6 · County need profile`) and a serif `h2` in words (the first sentence of the section); each figure tile is a layer target (`tabIndex=0`, focus ring): label (13 px) → value (30 px tabular when short, body size when it is a list or a weight) → `source · as of`; hidden values wear the one hatch (`data-slot=suppressed`); the section source line stays.
- `app/(app)/staff/packets/page.tsx`: an Info `Hint` beside the Variant and County labels (FilterBar pattern; the label keeps pointing at its select) and beside each download link; imports `components/packets/print.css` instead of the board sheet.
- `components/packets/print.css` (new, component-local): the DS-13 pattern copied, not imported: `@page { size: letter; margin; @bottom-center "Page n of N" }`, the fixed `packet-footer` as the Chromium fallback, shell and controls hidden, one ink, explain faces and the layer card hidden, each section on its own page, tiles never split; the F-403 packet rules move here out of `components/board/print.css` so each sheet has one owner.
- `tests/e2e/packets.spec.ts`: existing F-403/F-404 cases kept (the `h2` title assertion becomes the eyebrow assertion); one new case per criterion including a `-print` `emulateMedia` check.
- Out of scope: `app/(app)/staff/packets/loading.tsx` and the skeleton (PR #262); the O1 figure labels, which carry the instrument code from the stored row (`PHQ-9 mean change`) and are shared with the CSV and DOCX (open question 1); a packet history or stored packets (F-403 part 2).

## Audit (filled before code; before shots: supervisor, from production)
### Critique in brief
At 1024 the page reads as a form followed by a long document: two unlabelled selects, four buttons in a row, then a bordered cover and six sections whose `h2`s are nouns (`FAS results`), not findings. The figure tiles are the weakest part: a 13 px label, an 18 px value and a 12 px source line in a 12 px padded box, nothing on hover, a hidden value reads `fewer than 11` in the same weight as a real number. Nothing says what `Grant attachment` or `Board packet` means or what the downloads contain. The print sheet already breaks each section onto its own Letter page and repeats the footer, but shows no page numbers. Hierarchy within the cover and restraint (one accent, serif titles) pass.

## Exact copy
- Eyebrow: `Section 1 of 6 · County need profile`.
- Headline: the first sentence of the section (`Families referred by a court waited a median of 23 days for a first session.`); the remaining sentences and paragraphs follow as body.
- Terms: `Outcome change` · `How much families' survey scores moved on average between the start and end of therapy, on this instrument. A negative change on a distress scale means they felt better.` · `Families who improved` · `The share of families whose score on this instrument moved in the better direction between the start and end of therapy.` · `Average rate per hour` · `What FAS paid per hour of therapy, on average, to this group of providers this quarter.` · `Weight in the need score` · `How much this public measure counts toward the county need score, as a fraction of 1 and as a share of the whole.` · `Service counties` · `The Utah counties where FAS currently funds therapy, from FAS settings.` · fallback `A public measure for this county from the source named on the tile, in the release shown.`
- Hidden reasons (from `whyHidden`): `Fewer than 11 families, so the number is hidden to protect privacy.` · `Hidden so the smaller group next to it cannot be worked out from the total.` · `This number appears once the data behind it is set up.`
- Control hints: Variant `Grant attachment is for a funder and can be statewide or one county. Board packet is for the board and is always statewide.` · County `Limits the grant attachment to one Utah county. Counts under 11 families stay hidden.`
- Download hints: Word `A Word file of this packet: cover, six sections, figures and pictures. Dates are by month or quarter only and counts under 11 stay hidden.` · CSV `A spreadsheet of the figures only: one row each with label, value, source, as-of month and status, no paragraphs. Dates are by month or quarter only.`
- Print footer: unchanged `FAS Evidence · Counts under 11 are hidden · Oct 2026`; `Page n of N` from `@page`.

## States
Tile: value · hidden small count (hatch + card) · hidden complementary (hatch + card) · not available (hatch + card). Section: with figures · without (no `dl`). Print: the layer card, hints, tooltips and popovers never print.

## Trust rules
Every attribute comes from the same `Figure` the tile shows; the card adds no number. A hidden figure has no value row. Source and as-of are the tile's own `sourceLabel` and `asOf`. Headlines are the stored sentences, split, never rewritten. Nothing invented; the stored-packet card is not pretended.

## Accessibility
Tiles are `tabIndex=0` layer targets with the visible focus ring; hover, focus and Enter show the same words; Enter pins a `role="dialog"` named by the term; Escape closes and returns focus; arrow keys walk the tiles. Hints are 44 px below 1024 and read the same on hover, focus and tap. Sections are labelled by their headline. axe 0 serious or critical at 375, 1024 and 1440; `scrollWidth` ≤ 375.

## Decisions
- One `ExplainLayer` wraps all six sections rather than one per section, so one card and one live region serve the page and arrow keys walk every tile.
- The headline in words is the section's first sentence, split at the first sentence end before a capital letter, the same split the board Sources paragraph uses; the model is untouched so the DOCX and CSV keep their paragraphs.
- Hidden values keep their stored text (`fewer than 11`, `hidden`, `Not available yet`) inside the kit hatch rather than a glyph, so the page text still carries the words.
- The hints sit beside the labels and links as Info buttons (FilterBar precedent); the `<label>` keeps pointing at its select and the download stays a plain link.
- The packet print rules move out of `components/board/print.css` into the component-local sheet so the two pages no longer share one file; shared selectors are copied, not imported.

## Open questions
1. Outcome tiles are labelled with the instrument code from the stored row (`PHQ-9 mean change`); the instrument's full name exists on the measure (`label`) but the label is shared with the CSV and DOCX through `lib/packets/model.ts`, so renaming it is a model packet.
2. `Page n of N` renders only in engines that honour `@page` margin boxes; Chromium prints the fixed footer line without a number. A counted footer needs a paged-media engine or a server-side PDF, which is F-403 part 2.
3. No stored packet exists, so the cover still says `Prepared <month>` from the clock; a generated-date record would come with F-403 part 2.
4. `loading.tsx` for `/staff/packets` (out of scope above, waiting on #262): **resolved by DS-25.**
