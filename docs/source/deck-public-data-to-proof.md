# Deck (text version): From Public Data to Proof — How FAS Will Show Need and Impact
*Team briefing, October 2026. 16 slides. Speaker notes included. Source of the visual deck: CasePilot (claude.ai artifact, private).*

---
## 1. Cover
**From public data to proof.** How FAS will use FAS Evidence to turn free government data and our own case records into evidence funders trust.
> Notes: Two kinds of proof: that the need is real, and that our help works. FAS Evidence is the tracking system we are building for FAS.

## 2. Every funder asks us two questions
- **Is the need real?** Public data answers this: who is uninsured, where therapists are scarce, and how many families are in court. *(Proved with public data)*
- **Does our help work?** Only FAS's own records answer this: how fast families start, who finishes, what it costs, and how they improve. *(Proved with FAS's own data)*

## 3. Statement
**Public data shows where families struggle. Only FAS can show what happens next.**
> Notes: Government data stops at the courthouse door. What happens after a court order is something only FAS records.

## 4. Utah's need, in four numbers
- **62** mental health shortage areas across Utah
- **51%** of the mental health need in those areas is being met
- **24.2%** of Utah adults had 7+ poor mental health days in a month (2023)
- **265,351** new cases filed in Utah district courts in one year (FY2025)
*Sources: HRSA via KFF (as of Dec 31, 2025; 50.84% of need met, ~2.66M people, 81 more practitioners needed) · Utah DHHS IBIS-PH (2023) · Utah Courts 2026 Annual Report (FY2025; up from 256,588 in FY2024).*

## 5. Free data we can plug in
| Question | Free source |
|---|---|
| Who can't afford care? | Census uninsured counts, by county (SAHIE) |
| Where are therapists scarce? | Federal shortage-area list (HRSA) |
| Where is stress highest? | CDC neighborhood mental health estimates (PLACES) |
| Where do court families come from? | Utah Courts case filings, by county |
| Where do veterans live? | VA veteran counts, by county (VetPop2023) |
| Who funds this kind of work? | Federal grant lists and nonprofit tax filings (Grants.gov, USAspending, ProPublica) |
All free to use. Most refresh every year; the shortage list updates daily.

## 6. How the pieces fit together
**Free public data** (Census, HRSA, CDC, Utah Courts, VA, Grants.gov) → **FAS Evidence** (updates itself monthly, adds FAS case records, keeps families private) → **What our team sees** (county need map, weekly grant alerts, funder list, grant reports).
Once it's set up, no one re-types data. Updates run on their own.

## 7. What we'll build 1 of 4 — A need score for every Utah county
Inputs: how many people are uninsured (Census) · how short we are on therapists (HRSA) · how many adults report poor mental health (CDC) · how many families are in court (Utah Courts) → **County need score**, one number for each of Utah's 29 counties.
Use it to choose where FAS grows next, and back every grant application with county numbers.

## 8. What we'll build 2 of 4 — New grant matches, every week
1. Checks the federal grants list on its own
2. Keeps only grants that fit FAS: therapy access, court-involved families, veterans
3. Emails the team a short list every week
Bonus: see which Utah groups already won similar federal grants, and for how much (USAspending).
> Notes: Most FAS money may come through state agencies and foundations, so federal alerts cover part of the picture.

## 9. What we'll build 3 of 4 — A list of who funds work like ours
Step 1 Find groups like FAS (nonprofit tax filings) → Step 2 See who funds them (foundation filings list the grants they give, and to whom) → Step 3 Build our ask list.

## 10. What we'll build 4 of 4 — Grant reports in one click
A funder summary per county combining **public need data** with **FAS's own results**: pulls the need numbers from public data, adds FAS results automatically, exports a page funders can read. Built last, once our own data is clean and consistent.

## 12. Our headline number
**Days from a court order to a family's first therapy session.** Court order ●——[ ? days ]——● First session.
No public dataset tracks this. If we measure it carefully from day one, this proof belongs to FAS.

## 13. Measure progress the way Utah does
FAS families answer a short check-in survey when therapy starts and again later (OQ-45 adults · YOQ youth) **=** Utah's public system publishes yearly results from the same surveys (2025 Utah SUMH scorecards). Payoff: compare our families' progress to Utah's public system, apples to apples. Free alternative: PHQ-9 and GAD-7 (no Utah comparison).

## 14. Five rules we build in from day one
1. Report by county only — never addresses or exact dates
2. Hide any number under 11 — tiny counts can point to a real family
3. Keep dates, not court papers — juvenile court records are private by law
4. Leave out substance use details — those records carry extra federal protection
5. Be open about our sister clinic — show what goes to Connections Counseling and what goes to others
Before any provider shares session data with us, we get a legal review.
> Data use: Public and FAS data in FAS Evidence is used only (1) for FAS to measure and validate the problem it solves, and (2) to provide or attach that data in FAS's grant applications for government grants and through GrantAdvance. No resale, no paid product, no other use.
