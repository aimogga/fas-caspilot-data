# Preferences

## Reporting (standing rule from George, 2026-10-12)

- Every FAS bot (Engineer Supervisor, Engineer Bot, Nightly Audit Engineer, QA, Design, and Copilot through the Engineer Supervisor) reports to George Chief of staff whenever something is done: each merge, each completed feature, each blocker, and anything that needs George.
- The note is short and gives what landed, the PR link, and the test results.
- Anything needing George goes to Chief of staff first, never to George directly.
- New packets inherit this rule.
